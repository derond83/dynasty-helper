Offense. Values come from KeepTradeCut (1QB) or Dynasty Daddy, picked in the menu at the top. The other source is shown alongside for comparison. With the win-now setting above 0, each source's redraft values are blended in.

IDP. Neither source values defenders, so IDP value is built from Sleeper stats for the last three seasons, scored with this league's settings. This season counts most. A player's own big plays are mostly replaced by the position average for his snaps, so tackles and sacks drive value. His per-snap production is projected at the snaps he's playing now, short samples are pulled toward a replacement-level player, and value is discounted with age. It's then blended with the free FantasyPros dynasty DL / LB / DB rankings: the #k player there is worth what the model's #k player is worth. The unit is roughly points per game.

Moves. A roster keeps at least 2 QB, 3 RB, 3 WR, 2 TE, and 2 eligible players at each of DL, LB and DB (DL/LB and LB/DB players count for both), plus the IDP minimum from the tuning panel. Moves that fix a shortfall come first. At or above the IDP minimum, an IDP add replaces an IDP player, so offensive value isn't spent on defense. Offense and IDP are compared as a share of a typical league starter only for bench spots above that minimum. Taxi and IR players are never dropped, and neither is anyone you lock on the roster table.

Refreshing data. Your league loads live from Sleeper every time you open this page. If Sleeper can't be reached, the page falls back to a saved snapshot. A scheduled GitHub Action refreshes values, IDP stats and rankings once a day. To refresh sooner, run the "Refresh data and deploy" workflow from the repository's Actions tab.
